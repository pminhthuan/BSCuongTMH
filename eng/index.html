Clinic Director

MSc. Dr. Nguyen Quoc Cuong is currently an ENT specialist, working at the ENT Department, Da Nang Hospital for Women and Children.

He graduated as a General Physician and earned a Master's degree in ENT from Hue University of Medicine and Pharmacy.

He is a member of the Executive Committee of the Da Nang Young Physicians Association.

He regularly attends advanced training courses at home and abroad to keep up with the latest techniques and treatment methods.

With many years of experience in examination, treatment and surgery of ENT conditions, the doctor always aims to provide effective, safe treatment solutions tailored to each patient.

Expertise

  • Examination and treatment of ENT and respiratory conditions for children and adults.
  • Ear - Nose - Throat endoscopy using a Full HD system.
  • Early detection of abnormalities and cancers in the ENT region.
  • Treatment of otitis media, sinusitis and allergic rhinitis.
  • Treatment of tonsillitis, adenoiditis and other throat conditions.
  • Treatment of dizziness, tinnitus and hearing loss.
  • Consultation and follow-up before and after ENT surgery.
  • Removal of ENT foreign objects and ear cleaning treatment.

Services at the Clinic

The clinic provides examination, endoscopy, treatment and specialized care for ENT and respiratory conditions in children and adults. With a modern equipment system and direct examination by the doctor, patients receive an accurate diagnosis and advice on suitable treatment methods.

ENT Examination and Treatment

  • Ear pain, tinnitus, hearing loss.
  • Ear discharge.
  • Nasal congestion, runny nose.
  • Reduced or lost sense of smell.
  • Sore throat, difficulty swallowing.
  • Hoarseness.
  • Headache and dizziness caused by ENT conditions.
